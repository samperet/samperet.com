Driving to New Orleans during spring break
of junior year in college
was quite an unforgettable experience.
We showed up, three of us,
all from student government,
and found things to do.
We discovered this woman named Mama Dee
who was distributing cleaning supplies
and became a hub of information,
where we quickly became useful,
coordinating, organizing, and doing work.
We spent the week there cleaning out houses
that had been destroyed,
soaking with 18 inches of mud
for months at this point,
with everything just rotting.
We carried what few belongings we could find
to their owners
as they watched their whole world change.
The takeaway for me was sitting around a fire
with locals telling me
that just a few months before,
I would have been unwelcome
and shot at in that neighborhood.
And to have such incredible relationship bonds
during that time of incredible distress
has always been a fascination of mine.
Seeing the positive sides
of very seemingly unfortunate events like Katrina.
