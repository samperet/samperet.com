1 · The deck

Buttons, not apps.

A Stream Deck is a small panel of keys, each one a tiny screen. No phone to unlock, no app to find: the things your home does most sit on the wall or the coffee table, one press away.

  • Drawn for your home

    Every key gets its own icon and label, made for what it does in your house, not a stock grid of symbols.

  • Keys that show what’s on

    A key can show the heat, whether the garage is open, what’s playing, or that the laundry is done.

  • One press, a whole scene

    Movie night, dinner, goodnight: a single key runs every step, in order, the same way each time.

  • Pages for rooms and people

    A folder key opens a page for each room, and the kids can have a page of their own.

The deck runs on open-source software such as Home Assistant and Bitfocus Companion, from a computer or a small Raspberry Pi, so it keeps working without an account or a subscription.
